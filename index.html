💵 Event Pricing
These match the Field Trial Manager's pricing. Change them here if the club sets different rates — the totals, price tags and the data sent to the Manager all follow whatever is saved below.
Off = everyone pays the member rate. On = the Current Membership answer at the top of the form decides the tier, and buying a membership today counts as a member.
🌊 Water Races
Turn on to add the six water classes (Water Puppy, Water Junior, Water Senior, Water Old Dog, Water Powderpuff, Water Kids) alongside the drag classes on every dog entry. Leave off for a spring drag-only trial.
Water classes only come through on the Manager side if Water Races are switched on there too — the Manager ignores classes it isn't running.
📊 Google Sheets Connection
Registrations post to Google Sheets automatically when online. When offline, they queue and send the moment WiFi returns.
✓ Pre-configured — the Apps Script URL is already set. Tap Test Connection to verify it's working.

🪪 Recover Memberships
One-time tool. Re-sends the membership names already stored on this iPad (from the Submitted list) up to the Google Sheet, so the Field Trial Manager can pull them in. It only sends the memberships — it won't re-create dog entries. Safe to run more than once; the sheet skips any that are already there.
